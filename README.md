# 82 → 75 Plan

A personal gym and food plan as a small website: Workout A/B with form videos, same-muscle swaps,
looping exercise drawings, auto-saved set ticks and weights, a workout log, and an Iraqi meal plan.

- Open it on your phone, then use **Add to Home Screen** to get it as an app. It works offline.
- Ticks, weights and the log are saved in that phone's browser only (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere.

Credits: exercise drawings from [Everkinetic](https://github.com/everkinetic/data) by Greg Priday,

Form videos are links to YouTube.
