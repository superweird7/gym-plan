Start one thing at a time

Changing everything at once is how people quit. Add one new habit each week and keep the old ones.

  1. Week 1: drinks and gym

    Swap every cola and energy drink for zero sugar. Go to the gym 3 nights. Only 2 sets per exercise, light weights.

  2. Week 2: protein

    A palm of protein at every meal. Move up to 3 sets per exercise.

  3. Week 3: fast food

    Down to 2 nights a week, ordered the smart way (see Food).

  4. Week 4: walking

    15 minutes after lunch or dinner on rest days. Slowly build toward 7,000–8,000 steps a day.

Every Friday: check progress

  • Weigh yourself 3 mornings a week, after the toilet and before food. Compare weekly averages, not single days.
  • Measure your waist at the belly button once a week.
  • Every 2 weeks: a front and side photo, same mirror, same light.
  • Weekly average didn't drop for 2 weeks? Remove one bread or rice portion a day, or add 2,000 steps.

See a doctor if

  • You're still very tired after 4 weeks of zero-sugar drinks and steady sleep.
  • You feel a firm, rubbery lump behind a nipple, or it hurts.
  • You keep getting sick. Ask for vitamin D, iron, thyroid and testosterone tests.

This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice.

How every session works

  1. Warm up · 5 min

    Easy bike or walk, then 1 light set of the first exercise.

  2. Lift · about 40 min

    The 5 exercises below, in order.

  3. Finish · 10–15 min

    Incline walk (incline 4–6, speed 4–5 km/h) or bike. Weeks 1–2: 10 minutes. Cardio always comes last, never before lifting.

    Tired or short on time

    • Pair exercises 2 and 3 (chest and back). Do a set of one, rest 30 seconds, do a set of the other, then rest 2 minutes.
    • Eat your pre-gym snack 30–60 minutes before, and bring water.
    • Energy drink only before the gym, never after. Caffeine late makes your sleep worse.

    Missed a night or got sick

    Neck ruleSymptoms only above the neck (runny nose, light sore throat): a light workout is OK. Below the neck (fever, body aches, cough): rest.
    • First workout back: a half workout, 2 sets with lighter weights.
    • Never miss twice in a row. After one missed night, the next session happens, even as a mini workout.
    • Mini workout (20 min): the first 3 exercises, 2 sets each, no cardio.
    • Bored? Try to beat last time's weight in the kg box. After 8 weeks, switch 1–2 exercises to their Swap.
